Security Policy
==================

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in IoTGateway, please report it privately:

- By email: [peter@abc4.io](mailto:peter@abc4.io)
- Online: [https://abc4.io/Feedback.md](https://waher.se/Feedback.md)

Please include enough information to understand and reproduce the issue, such as the affected 
component or version, reproduction steps, potential impact, and any suggested fix.

## Responsible Disclosure

Responsible disclosure is strongly recommended. Please do not publish or otherwise disclose a 
vulnerability before the maintainers have had sufficient time to investigate and fix it. 
Unless otherwise agreed, please allow at least 45 days after reporting the vulnerability 
before publishing any details.

Thank you for helping keep IoTGateway and its users secure.
